    FAQ

    Frequently
    asked questions

    +What is Glossa?

    A free Chrome extension for Netflix. Pause, click any word in the subtitle, and see its meaning in English, Hindi and Hinglish. When the film ends, you get a vocabulary PDF.

    +Does it need English subtitles?

    Yes. Words come only from English subtitles. If a film has them, Glossa switches them on for you, and you can keep them hidden on screen.

    +How much does it cost?

    Nothing. Glossa is free and open source under the MIT license.

    +What is sent over the internet?

    Only the word or line you look up, to Free Dictionary API, Wiktionary, MyMemory and Datamuse. Your subtitles and watching history stay in your browser.

    +Does it work on phones or TVs?

    Not yet. It works on Netflix in a desktop browser: Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc or Opera.

    +How do I install it?

    Download glossa.zip and unzip it. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the glossa folder.
