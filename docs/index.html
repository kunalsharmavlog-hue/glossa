How it works
01 / Watch

Turn on English subtitles and press play.

SubVocab quietly saves every subtitle line while the movie plays. A green REC badge on the icon shows it is listening. If the subtitles are off, it switches English on for you and keeps them hidden if you prefer.

02 / Catch

The hard words are caught for you.

Every line is checked against a list of everyday English words. Common words are skipped, and so are names, sounds and filler. You choose how hard the list should be.

03 / Pause & Pick

Pause, and the subtitle turns into text you can pick.

Click one word, or drag across a phrase like "piece of cake" or the whole line. Its meaning appears right there, with Hindi, Hinglish and a 🔊 button to hear it. Press ★ to keep it.

04 / Keep

The movie ends. Your PDF is ready.

Your picks come first, then every hard word with its meaning and the exact line it came from, with a timestamp. The PDF downloads by itself.

    Features

    Learn the words you actually hear.

    ⏸

    Pause & Pick: any word, any phrase, the whole line.

    Didn't get what a character said? Pause and select it. One word gives you its meaning and similar words. A phrase like "give up" or "piece of cake" gives you the idiom's meaning. A whole line is translated to Hindi, with the hard words explained.

    🇮🇳

    Hindi + Hinglish

    Every meaning comes with Hindi in Devanagari and in Roman letters, so it clicks even if you don't read Hindi script.

    🎬

    The real line, with a timestamp

    Each word comes with the line it was spoken in, so you remember the scene and not just the definition.

    🎚️

    Pick your level

    All hard words, medium and hard, or only the hardest. Easy words never fill up your list.

    🔊

    Hear it said

    Tap Listen on any card to hear how the word or phrase is pronounced.

    📄

    An automatic PDF

    Finish the movie and a clean PDF lands in your Downloads folder. Episodes are named with season and episode.

    🔒

    Private by design

    No account and no server of ours. Subtitles stay in your browser. Only the words you look up go to free dictionary services.

    💸

    Free and open source

    The whole extension is on GitHub. Read the code, change it, share it.

    The PDF

    A vocabulary list from every movie.

    Revise on your phone on the way to work. Print it. Share it with a friend who is learning English too.

    • Your picks first. The words you saved during the movie lead the list.
    • Short meanings. One clear sense per word, written for learners where possible.
    • Similar words. Simpler words that mean the same thing.
    • Names left out. Character names and places are filtered automatically.

    Install in one minute

    Four steps. Then just watch.

    SubVocab is not on the Chrome Web Store yet, so you add it yourself. It works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc.

    Download

    Get subvocab.zip and unzip it. Keep the folder somewhere safe.

    Open extensions

    Type chrome://extensions in the address bar.

    Developer mode

    Turn on the Developer mode switch in the top right.

    Load unpacked

    Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder. Pin the icon.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    Does it need English subtitles?

    Yes. Words are collected only from English subtitles. If the movie has them, SubVocab switches them on for you. You can keep them hidden on screen and they still get read.

    Does it work on other streaming sites?

    Right now it works on Netflix in a desktop browser. It does not work in the phone or TV apps.

    Is my watching history sent anywhere?

    No. Subtitle lines are kept in your browser's own storage. Only the words and phrases being looked up are sent to free dictionary and translation services (Free Dictionary API, Wiktionary, MyMemory and Datamuse).

    Can I make a PDF before the movie ends?

    Yes. Open the SubVocab popup and press "Create PDF now" for any recent movie. You can also rename the PDF's headline there.

    Does it cost anything?

    No. It is free and open source.
