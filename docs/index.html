    Install

    Ready in about a minute

    Glossa is not on the Chrome Web Store yet, so you add it in four quick steps.

    Download

    Download glossa.zip and unzip it.

    Open extensions

    Go to chrome://extensions.

    Turn on Developer mode in the top-right corner.

    Load unpacked

    Click Load unpacked, pick the glossa folder and pin the icon.

    FAQ

    Frequentlyasked questions

    Is Glossa really free?

    Yes. It is free and open source under the MIT license. There is no paid plan.

    Does it need English subtitles?

    Yes. Words come only from English subtitles. If a movie has them, Glossa switches them on for you, and you can keep them hidden on screen.

    Does it work on my phone or TV?

    Not yet. It works on Netflix in a desktop browser: Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc or Opera.

    What is sent over the internet?

    Only the word or line you look up, to Free Dictionary API, Wiktionary, MyMemory and Datamuse. Your subtitles and watching history stay in your browser.

    Can I make the PDF before the movie ends?

    Yes. Open the Glossa popup and press "Create PDF now" for any recent movie. You can rename its headline there too.

    How do I get started?

    Download glossa.zip, load it in chrome://extensions, then play anything on Netflix with English subtitles. That's it.
